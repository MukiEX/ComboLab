Research bot

Plays combos by itself in Training mode and sweeps every step frame by frame, filling in exact windows and sweet spots. Before starting: dummy on No guard, Drive and Super gauges on infinite / auto-recover, life auto-recover on, Counter setting on Punish Counter for PC combos, and keep your hands off the controller (touching it stops the bot). Playing offline is safest.
Shift-click or Ctrl-click combos in the list to profile a batch. “All combos” skips anything already at 100% and gives up on an experimental combo after 30 attempts in a row without a success.
